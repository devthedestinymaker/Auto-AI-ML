# Deep Learning

## What you will learn

Understand neural networks as trainable functions and gain practical experience with their training and evaluation.

## Important concepts

- Tensors, layers, activations, and forward passes
- Loss functions, gradients, backpropagation, and optimizers
- Batches, epochs, learning rates, and regularization
- Convolutional and sequence-model basics
- Generalization, validation curves, and reproducibility

## Practical exercises

- Implement a tiny neural network calculation with plain Python to trace its parts.
- Train a small model on a standard, appropriately licensed educational dataset.
- Compare learning curves under different learning rates and regularization settings.

## Suggested projects

- An image classifier with a documented error analysis.
- A small sequence prediction experiment compared against a simple baseline.

Record training settings and compare against simpler models; more layers do not guarantee better results.
